Unit 1 introduces the idea of community: family, neighbourhood, and the institutions around us.

Key concepts: social values, duties of a citizen, and respect for diversity in caste, language, and religion.

Map skill: locate your district headquarters and two neighbouring districts on the map of Nepal.

Activity: interview an elder about how your village solved drinking-water problems ten years ago.

Exam tip: expect one short question on community values and one map or chart question from this unit.

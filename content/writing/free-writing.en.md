---
title: "Free Writing: Write Without Fear"
date: "2026-09-04"
subsection: "free-writing"
---

Free writing means writing whatever comes to mind, without worrying about grammar or spelling.

Set a timer for ten minutes and keep your pen moving. Do not stop to correct mistakes.

The goal is fluency first. Accuracy comes later, when you revise your draft.

In class, I ask students to free-write about their village, then underline three sentences they like.

Those three sentences often become the start of a guided paragraph the next day.

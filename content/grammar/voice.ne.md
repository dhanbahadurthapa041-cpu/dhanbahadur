वाक्यमा क्रिया (Verb) को प्रयोग कर्तामुखी छ वा कर्ममुखी छ भन्ने कुरा **Voice** (वाच्य) ले निर्धारण गर्छ।
- **Active Voice (कर्तृवाच्य):** जब वाक्यको कर्ताले आफैँ काम गर्छ।
- **Passive Voice (कर्मवाच्य):** जब वाक्यमा काम गर्ने व्यक्तिभन्दा कामको परिणाम वा कर्म (Object) मुख्य हुन्छ।

### १. Active बाट Passive बनाउने नियमहरू
१. Active वाक्यको कर्म (Object) लाई Passive वाक्यको कर्ता (Subject) बनाइन्छ।
२. काल (Tense) अनुसार सहायक क्रिया (**be**: is, am, are, was, were, been, being) थपिन्छ।
३. मुख्य क्रियालाई अनिवार्य रूपमा **तेस्रो रूप (V3 - Past Participle)** मा बदल्नुपर्छ।
४. कर्ताको अगाडि **by** राखेर पछाडि राखिन्छ (अस्पष्ट कर्ता भए हटाउन सकिन्छ)।

### २. काल अनुसार सहायक क्रिया
- **Simple Present (V1/V5):** is / am / are + V3
  - *Active:* Ram plays football. $\rightarrow$ *Passive:* Football is played by Ram.
- **Present Continuous:** is / am / are + being + V3
  - *Active:* She is washing clothes. $\rightarrow$ *Passive:* Clothes are being washed.
- **Simple Past (V2):** was / were + V3
  - *Active:* He wrote a letter. $\rightarrow$ *Passive:* A letter was written by him.
- **Present Perfect:** has / have + been + V3
  - *Active:* We have painted the gate. $\rightarrow$ *Passive:* The gate has been painted.
- **Modals (can, must, should):** modal + be + V3

### ५+ उदाहरणहरू
१. Active: The teacher solved the difficult math problem.
   Passive: The difficult math problem was solved by the teacher. (शिक्षकद्वारा समस्या समाधान गरियो।)
२. Active: Farmers grow golden paddy in the plains.
   Passive: Golden paddy is grown in the plains by farmers.
३. Active: The school is renovating the classrooms.
   Passive: The classrooms are being renovated by the school.
४. Active: Rita has submitted the completed report.
   Passive: The completed report has been submitted by Rita.
५. Active: Everyone should respect the national flag.
   Passive: The national flag should be respected.
६. Active: They opened the health post last month.
   Passive: The health post was opened last month.

### अभ्यास (Practice Exercise)
तलका वाक्यहरूलाई Passive Voice मा रूपान्तरण गर्नुहोस्:
१. The librarian cataloged fifty new books.
२. Diligent scouts are cleaning the school grounds.
३. The government will construct a bridge here.
४. Parents have praised the school examination results.
५. You must lock the computer lab before evening.

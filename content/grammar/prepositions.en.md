A preposition is a word placed before a noun or pronoun to show time, location, direction, or relationship with another part of the sentence. Mastering prepositions is key to fluent English communication.

### 1. Prepositions of Time (In, On, At)
- **At** is used for precise clock times and festive occasions: *at 6:00 AM*, *at midnight*, *at Dashain*.
- **On** is used for specific days and dates: *on Sunday*, *on 15th August*, *on my birthday*.
- **In** is used for longer periods like months, years, seasons, and parts of the day: *in July*, *in 2026*, *in winter*, *in the morning*.

### 2. Prepositions of Place (At, On, In)
- **At** denotes a specific point or address: *at the bus stop*, *at the entrance*, *at home*.
- **On** denotes contact with a surface: *on the table*, *on the wall*, *on the second floor*.
- **In** denotes being inside an enclosed space or large geographical area: *in the classroom*, *in Kathmandu*, *in Nepal*.

### 3. Prepositions of Movement and Direction
- **To**: indicates a destination ("He walked to school").
- **Into**: indicates movement from outside to inside ("She jumped into the river").
- **Through**: indicates passing within a space from one end to another ("We walked through the dense forest").
- **Across**: moving from one side to the opposite side ("The dog ran across the road").

### Illustrative Examples
1. The morning assembly begins promptly **at** 10:00 AM.
2. The final examination starts **on** Monday morning.
3. Our school is situated **in** a peaceful village of Surkhet.
4. The teacher placed the attendance register **on** the principal's desk.
5. The frightened cat darted **into** the dark room.
6. They walked together **through** the green fields after school.

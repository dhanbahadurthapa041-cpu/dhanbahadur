title: "Articles: A, An, and The"

Articles are words that define a noun as specific or unspecific. English has two types of articles: indefinite articles (**a**, **an**) and the definite article (**the**).

### 1. Indefinite Articles: A and An
Use **a** and **an** only with singular countable nouns mentioned for the first time.
- Use **a** before words beginning with a consonant sound: *a pen*, *a dog*, *a university* (the sound is /juː/).
- Use **an** before words beginning with a vowel sound: *an orange*, *an umbrella*, *an hour* (silent 'h'), *an honest man*.

### 2. Definite Article: The
Use **the** before singular or plural nouns when the speaker and listener already know which item is being referred to, or when the noun is unique.
- Before unique natural objects: *the sun*, *the moon*, *the earth*.
- Before names of mountain ranges, rivers, and oceans: *the Himalayas*, *the Karnali river*, *the Pacific Ocean*.
- Before superlative adjectives: *the tallest boy*, *the most beautiful flower*.
- When a noun is repeated: "I saw a stray dog. **The** dog was barking loudly."

### 3. Omission of Articles (Zero Article)
Do not use an article with:
- Uncountable or plural nouns used in a general sense: "Children love playing games." / "Knowledge is power."
- Names of languages, meals, or individual peaks: *English*, *breakfast*, *Mount Everest*.

### Illustrative Examples
1. Rita bought **an** umbrella because it was raining.
2. He is studying at **a** university in Pokhara.
3. **The** sun rises in the east and sets in the west.
4. Sagarmatha is **the** highest peak in the world.
5. We enjoyed **a** delicious meal at **the** new restaurant in our village.
6. Honest workers always earn **the** respect of everyone.

### Practice Exercise
Fill in the blanks with **a**, **an**, **the**, or **—** (no article):
1. My brother is _______ engineer in Kathmandu.
2. Look at _______ bird singing on that branch.
3. She stayed at hotel for _______ hour before leaving.
4. _______ Mt. Everest is located in Nepal.
5. He borrowed _______ book from our school library.

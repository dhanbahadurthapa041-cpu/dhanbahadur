---
title: "Articles: A, An and The"
date: "2026-09-04"
subsection: "articles"
---

Use "a" before a consonant sound: a book, a school, a university (sounds like "yu").

Use "an" before a vowel sound: an apple, an hour (the h is silent), an honest man.

Use "the" when both speaker and listener know which one: "Close the door." / "The sun rises in the east."

No article for general plurals: "Teachers work hard." But "The teachers of this school" is specific.

---
title: "Tense: Present Simple vs Past Simple"
date: "2026-09-04"
subsection: "tense"
---

Use the present simple for habits and facts: "She walks to school." / "Water boils at 100°C."

Use the past simple for finished events: "She visited Pokhara last year."

Signal words help: every day, always, usually point to present; yesterday, last week, ago point to past.

Common mistake: "I am agree" is wrong. Say "I agree" (present) or "I agreed" (past).

Practice: write five sentences about your daily routine, then rewrite them as what you did yesterday.

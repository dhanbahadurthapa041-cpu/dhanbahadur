कुनै व्यक्तिले भनेको कुरालाई जस्ताको तस्तै नभनी आफ्ना शब्दमा अरूलाई बताउने तरिकालाई **Reported Speech** (Indirect Speech वा अप्रत्यक्ष कथन) भनिन्छ।

### १. वाक्य रूपान्तरणका मुख्य नियमहरू
१. **Reporting Verb को परिवर्तन:** *said to* पछाडि कर्म (Object) भए *told* बनाइन्छ। प्रश्नवाचक वाक्यमा *asked* प्रयोग हुन्छ।
२. **संयोजक (Conjunction):** साधारण वाक्यमा उद्धरण चिह्न हटाएर **that** राखिन्छ। 'हजुर/हैन' (Yes/No) प्रश्नमा **if** वा **whether** प्रयोग हुन्छ।
३. **सर्वनाम (Pronoun) को नियम:** प्रथम पुरुष (I, We) Reporting verb को कर्ता अनुसार र द्वितीय पुरुष (You) कर्म अनुसार बदलिन्छ।

### २. कालको परिवर्तन (Tense Change)
यदि Reporting verb भूतकाल (Said) मा भए:
- Simple Present $\rightarrow$ Simple Past (V2)
- Present Continuous $\rightarrow$ Past Continuous (was/were)
- Simple Past / Present Perfect $\rightarrow$ Past Perfect (had + V3)
- Will / Can $\rightarrow$ Would / Could
*अपवाद:* सार्वभौमिक सत्य (Universal truth) मा काल बदलिँदैन।

### ३. समय र दूरी सूचक शब्दहरू
- *today* $\rightarrow$ *that day*, *yesterday* $\rightarrow$ *the day before*, *tomorrow* $\rightarrow$ *the next day*, *now* $\rightarrow$ *then*।

### ५+ उदाहरणहरू
१. Direct: Binod said, "I am tired."
   Indirect: Binod said that he was tired. (बिनोदले आफू थकित भएको बताए।)
२. Direct: Sita said to Ramesh, "I finished my work."
   Indirect: Sita told Ramesh that she had finished her work.
३. Direct: The teacher said, "Light travels fast."
   Indirect: The teacher said that light travels fast.
४. Direct: Mohan said to me, "Do you know him?"
   Indirect: Mohan asked me if I knew him.
५. Direct: She asked, "Where is your school?"
   Indirect: She asked me where my school was.
६. Direct: Father said, "Work hard."
   Indirect: Father advised me to work hard.

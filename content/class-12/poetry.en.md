title: "Class 12 English: How to Read a Poem"
date: "2026-09-04"
subsection: "poetry"
---

Poetry in Class 12 asks you to feel first, then analyse. Read the poem aloud twice before touching the questions.

Step one: who speaks, and to whom? Step two: which image repeats — light, road, river, bird?

Step three: find one figure of speech (simile, metaphor, personification) and explain its effect in one line.

Seen texts come from your textbook; unseen poems test the same skill on a new piece.

Practice: take any two-quatrain poem, write its theme in twenty words, then compare with a friend's version.
